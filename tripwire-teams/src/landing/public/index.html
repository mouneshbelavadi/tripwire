How Tripwire Works

Hybrid teams waste hours in morning traffic uncertainty. Tripwire replaces awkward channel polls with an automated 3-step consensus engine.

STEP 01

Team leads plan an office day in Slack

Using the /tripwire plan command, the lead picks the date, target office arrival time, and a morning decision cutoff (e.g., 07:30 AM). Tripwire connects to the team's Google Calendar event.

/tripwire plan Date: Tomorrow (Thursday) Office: RMZ Ecoworld, Outer Ring Rd Arrival: 09:30 AM | Decision: 07:30 AM
STEP 02

Tripwire monitors corridors & weather

We query predicted and live traffic corridors using approximate neighborhood areas (~1 km resolution). No GPS tracking. No battery drain. We also check Open-Meteo for heavy precipitation.

Checking 6 commute corridors... Live duration vs. 24h baseline: • Indiranagar -> Tech Park: +38m • HSR Layout -> Tech Park: +45m Rain probability: 80%
STEP 03

Two consecutive checks trigger the remote call

If disruption exceeds threshold on two consecutive source-fresh checks, the lead gets an actionable Slack card. One click updates the Google Calendar invite to remote with Google Meet.

✓ Check 1 verified (07:15) ✓ Check 2 verified (07:25) [ Make it Remote with Google Meet ] -> Updated Calendar for 6 attendees
System Invariants

Engineered For Zero Flapping & Absolute Trust

Automations fail when they cry wolf or guess blindly. Tripwire is governed by mathematical system invariants that guarantee reliable decisions.

🔄

Two Consecutive Fresh Checks

A single traffic blip never triggers a remote call. Disruption must be confirmed across two consecutive checks within a 10-minute freshness window before a recommendation is posted.

❓

"Unknown" Is Never "Fine"

If an API fails or returns stale data, it is marked as unknown. It never resets a pending breach silently. If more than 30% of team readings are unknown, Tripwire outputs "cannot assess" instead of guessing.

👤

Single Decision Maker

No endless emoji voting or consensus gridlock. Only the plan owner who initiated the office day holds the button to confirm the remote switch or keep the office day.

🛡️

Privacy by Design (No GPS)

Home locations are stored only as a general neighborhood label rounded to 2 decimal places (~1 km) and encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Leads only see aggregates (e.g. "4 of 7 affected"), never home maps.

Honest Privacy

What Data We Collect (And What We Don't)

We believe workplace tools should never monitor your private life. Here is our full, transparent data ledger.

Data Point Status How Tripwire Uses It Storage & Protection
Approximate Home Neighborhood ✓ Stored Converted to rounded coordinates (~1 km radius) to query commute corridors. AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest. Never shown to colleagues or managers.
Commute Mode & Limit ✓ Stored Your preferred travel mode (Car, Two-Wheeler, Transit) and max tolerable minutes. Stored in PostgreSQL with workspace isolation.
Slack User ID & Email ✓ Stored To post interactive cards and match attendee emails to the Google Calendar event. Purged immediately on /tripwire forget.
Real-Time GPS Tracking ✕ NEVER We do not track live devices, mobile apps, or continuous movement. Zero background geolocation. We never know where you are.
Exact Street Address ✕ NEVER Tripwire never asks for or stores house numbers, apartment names, or exact streets. Truncated to 2 decimal coordinates (~1 km bounding area).
Slack Messages & Chats ✕ NEVER Tripwire only listens to its own slash commands and explicit mentions. No channel message logging or scanning. Ever.

The One-Command Privacy Purge

Want to remove your details at any time? Simply run /tripwire forget in Slack. Tripwire immediately and permanently deletes your home coordinates, commute settings, and history from our database.
